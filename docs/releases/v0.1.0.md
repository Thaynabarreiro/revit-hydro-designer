## 🚀 Overview

`v0.1.0 - Cold Water MVP` delivers a complete, end-to-end open-source cold water plumbing design engine inside Autodesk Revit (tested on Revit 2027). It automates fixture extraction, daily demand calculation, reservoir volume determination, weighted fixture unit design flow, 3D pipe network modeling, pressure verification via Fair-Whipple-Hsiao, and calculation report generation.

---

## ✨ Implemented Features

### 🔍 M0 — Model Health Audit
- Automated verification of Revit pipe types, routing preferences, piping systems, plumbing families, parameters, and orphan views.

### 📖 M1 — Architectural Reader & Spatial Clustering
- Extraction of plumbing fixtures and room boundaries from linked architectural BIM models.
- Spatial proximity clustering combining multi-component fixture symbols into unified consumption points.

### 📐 M2–M4 — Cold Water Sizing Engine (NBR 5626)
- Daily water demand calculation based on occupancy and building typology.
- Two-day reserve volume calculation (60% lower / 40% upper reservoir distribution).
- Design flow calculation using weighted fixture unit method ($Q = 0.3 \sqrt{\Sigma P}$).
- Water meter sizing and service line diameter selection.

### 📦 M5 — MEP Fixture Family Placement
- Automated placement of cold water MEP plumbing families at exact level elevations.

### 🛠️ M6 — 3D Pipe Network Modeling
- Main-and-branch 3D pipe network routing (riser, main spine, perpendicular branches, wall drops).
- 90° fitting connections joining every placed fixture to the active piping system.

### ⚡ M9 — Fair-Whipple-Hsiao Head Loss & Pressure Verification
- Pressure verification considering static head, friction loss (Fair-Whipple-Hsiao), and fitting equivalent lengths.
- Iterative pipe diameter adjustments to guarantee positive pressure head margins at all fixtures.

### 📄 M8 — Print-Ready Calculation Report
- Automated generation of comprehensive calculation reports in HTML, PDF, and Word (`.docx`).

### 🎛️ Studio BIM Native WPF UI
- Light-themed WPF Studio window embedded directly inside Revit with tab switching and instant calculation action buttons.
